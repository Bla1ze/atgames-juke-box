Jukebox

A music and internet-radio player for your pinball cabinet. Tune into thousands of radio stations, play your own music, videos and YouTube, and watch the album art (or the video) light up the backglass while a jukebox marquee glows on the DMD.

Playing your music

Jukebox scans a folder named music at the root of the USB drive, and every folder inside it.

  1. On a computer, open the USB drive.
  2. Create a folder named music (all lowercase) at the drive's root.
  3. Copy your music into it. An Artist/Album/track layout works great.
  4. Plug the drive into the cabinet and open My Music: browse by Albums, Artists or All Tracks, or turn on Shuffle. Or skip the copying: Add music over Wi-Fi sends music from a browser.

Album art comes from a cover, folder or front image in the album's folder, or a picture embedded in MP3 and FLAC files. Names come from the files' tags, or from the folders when there are none.

Get started

  1. Download jukebox-app.zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it into a folder named jukebox-app inside the external folder on your cabinet's USB stick: external/jukebox-app/. From 2.2 on, Jukebox updates itself; for older versions, replace the old files. Your favorites carry over.
  3. Start Jukebox from the cabinet's External Applications menu.
  4. For your own music, add a music folder at the root of the USB drive (see above). Radio and Search need the cabinet online.

Questions & help

The basics. The README has the full details.

Which cabinets does it work on?

AtGames Legends Pinball 4KP and HDP. Jukebox reads the cabinet model to put the menu, the backglass art and the DMD marquee on the right screens.

Does it need the internet?

Radio, Search and YouTube do. My Music and Videos work offline.

Do I need the Arcade Control Panel?

No. Everything works with the cabinet's own buttons; a flipper saves favorites. Y and X still work if you have a panel.

It says "No music found".

Check that the folder is named exactly music (lowercase), sits at the root of the USB drive, and holds files of a supported type: MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus, WAV or WMA. A large library shows "Scanning library…" briefly the first time. More in the Your music guide.

My album art doesn't show.

Put a cover.jpg (or folder / front, .jpg or .png) in the album's folder. Pictures embedded in MP3 and FLAC files are read too, but not yet those inside M4A/AAC files.

A radio station won't play.

Stations come and go, so it may simply be offline right now. Try another one.

Something shows on the wrong screen.

Create a file named jukebox.cfg at the root of the USB stick containing diag = 1. Jukebox then labels each screen and writes jukebox-panels.log next to the file. Open an issue with that log.

How does YouTube work?

Choose YouTube on the main menu: Search YouTube, or pick Lofi Girl (its 24/7 live radios), NewRetroWave, NoCopyrightSounds or NPR Music. The video plays on the backglass with Now Playing on the playfield, and the rest of the list follows. YouTube plays through yt-dlp, a free, public-domain tool that isn't part of Jukebox: the first time, Jukebox asks, downloads it from GitHub (about 3 MB) and keeps it updated weekly. It needs the internet. Jukebox isn't affiliated with YouTube or the channels it lists.

How do I play videos?

Make a folder named videos at the root of the USB drive, copy video files into it (sub-folders are fine), and choose Videos on the main menu, the strip under the tiles. The video plays on the backglass with Now Playing on the playfield, and the rest of the folder follows. H.264 up to 1080p at 30 fps plays best; 4K, 60 fps or HEVC files may stutter.

Can I add music or videos without taking the USB drive out?

Yes. In My Music, choose Add music and videos over Wi-Fi. The cabinet shows an address and a PIN: open the address in a browser on a computer or phone on the same network, enter the PIN, and drop in songs, videos or whole album folders. Videos go to the videos folder. Press Back on the cabinet when you're done. See the Your music guide.

How do I update Jukebox?

From version 2.2, Jukebox checks for a new version when it starts. When there is one, a banner appears under the main menu tiles: select it, choose Install, then Restart now. Only the app itself is replaced. On 2.1 or older, install 2.2 by hand once (see Get started).

Will an update keep my favorites?

Yes. Favorites are saved on the device and carry over when you replace the app with a new version.
