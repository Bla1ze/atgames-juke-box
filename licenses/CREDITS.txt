# JUKEBOX
Internet radio, your own music and videos, and
YouTube on your cabinet. A free fan project by
Bla1ze. Not affiliated with or endorsed by
AtGames.

# WHERE THE MUSIC COMES FROM
Jukebox doesn't host or own any of what it plays.
- Radio stations come from the community
  Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info).
  Each station belongs to its broadcaster.
- YouTube videos and live streams belong to
  their creators. Jukebox isn't affiliated with
  YouTube or Google, or with the channels it
  lists (Lofi Girl, NewRetroWave,
  NoCopyrightSounds, NPR Music).
- Your own music and videos stay on your USB
  drive.
